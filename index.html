Launch an app

Your page stays static. The hosts you pay serve its data.

Choose who can write

Pick one rule for your app's data. Every host enforces it, and it can't change after launch.

// who may write which keys

What hosts can and can't do

Every host you pay keeps a copy. Browsers check what they read, and every host enforces your rules.

Reads are checked

Every value is checked against your app's chain. Read from two or more hosts and a value shows only when two agree; one host alone could still serve old data.

Rules hold everywhere

A write your rules refuse is refused by every host, so no single host can let it through.

Host records are on the chain

Each host's serving record, stake and domain, read from the chain before you pay.

Sell hosting

Run hearth sell next to your Lattice node (5.0.0 or newer). You set a daily price; it serves each app that pays.

  • Apps pay up front, before you serve anything
  • Spend what you earn on hosting and storage, or sell it with hearth sell-coin
  • Your machine makes each app's blocks, so no miners are needed
  • Without a domain, you're listed under your key
  • Optionally burn coin with hearth burn; buyers see how much

What does it cost?

Each host sets its own daily price in Hearth coin. You pay up front for the days you choose, and renew only the hosts that kept serving. Visitors pay nothing.

How do I get Hearth coin?

Buy it on Lattice's own exchange, from the Buy coin page. Hosts sell what they earn there, and Lattice checks both sides of every trade.

What happens if a host stops?

The other hosts you hired keep serving. You stop paying that host when its days run out.

Can my app run SQL or server code?

No. Data is keys and values, read by key or prefix. There are no server functions.

Is my app's data private?

No. Anyone can read an app's data. Encrypt what should stay private before you write it.

Can I change my app's rules later?

No. They are fixed at launch.

Why do hosts put coin at stake?

A host may burn coin it can never get back; you see how much before you pay. It says nothing about how well a host serves; its record does.

Is Hearth live on mainnet?

Yes. It runs on Lattice mainnet, and hosts are paid in the market's own coin.
